COMPANY OVERVIEW

About YogTeck Business Solution

YogTeck Business Solution is an IT and software development company based in Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh (Registered Office: 302/2, Mangla Vihar 2, New PAC Line, Kanpur Nagar, UP 208015). We specialize in custom website development, enterprise ERP platforms, GST billing software, mobile applications, and digital growth systems for enterprises, manufacturers, and startups in Kanpur and across India.

Our Core Mission

To empower traditional Indian manufacturers, traders, and growing direct-to-consumer brands with enterprise-grade cloud software, high-converting websites, and automated operations that eliminate human error and drive lasting business profitability.

Engineering Philosophy

We reject bloated templates, hidden licensing fees, and vendor lock-in. Every solution we build is engineered for high performance, sub-second response times, stringent data security, and 100% intellectual property ownership handed over to you.

Local Presence & Real Support

Headquartered in Kanpur Nagar, we understand the local manufacturing cycles of leather, textiles, chemical trade, and wholesale distribution. We provide in-person discovery, on-site staff training, and fast remote helpdesk support.

Company Background & Story

YogTeck Business Solution was founded with a clear focus: bridging the gap between high-end digital technology and practical MSME business operations in Uttar Pradesh and North India. Having partnered with enterprise leaders in real estate (MMR Constructions), healthcare e-commerce (Yogkart Healthcare), and technology distribution (Business Web Solutions), we continue to engineer scalable platforms that transform offline business workflows into competitive online assets.
